## Why the classifier gate remains

The [2026-11-20 reply](susr-geography-clarification.md) is authoritative for source
identity and identifier/hierarchy guidance, but the responding unit explicitly
declined to determine questions 2–6: exact-file CC BY applicability, transformation,
commercial reuse, attribution and automated acquisition. It advised referral to
competent expert departments. General public terms support further inquiry, not
an assertion that the unit answered those questions affirmatively.

The maintainer confirmed a follow-up sent as a reply on **2026-11-27**:
`SENT_AWAITING_REPLY`, day precision. The competent recipient/forwarding has not
been established. No specialist answer or permission is claimed, and the agent
sent no correspondence.

## Acquisition and release gates

This milestone deliberately remains **manual acquisition only** regardless of
the more qualified current general automation wording. The maintainer must use
the [official catalogue](https://zber.statistics.sk/metaudaje/ciselniky) and supply
the exact files and actual acquisition metadata privately. Hashes prove capture
integrity, not permission or a cryptographic publisher signature.

A future `SUSR_TECHNICAL_FULL_REPLACEMENT_READY` result would establish technical
reproducibility only. Production remains `RIGHTS_CONFIRMATION_PENDING` until
classifier-specific applicability is directly resolved by a competent unit or
equivalent authoritative evidence is established. Then a separate, deliberately
scoped migration can be considered; it is not queued automatically. Technical
failure should instead record the exact blocker and park the migration.

PSC's separate `NO_GO_SUCCESSOR_PSC` gate and the current PortalVS production
provenance are unaffected. The developer pilot can proceed independently without
claims of stable release, unrestricted redistribution or official endorsement.
